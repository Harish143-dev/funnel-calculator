# funnel-calculator

Her Will funnel calculator: type the ad budget and funnel rates to see subscriptions, revenue, cost per subscription and the budget needed for the subscription goal.

- Open `index.html` in a browser (Safari, Chrome). It starts on the Expected scenario, the same numbers as the Her Will Growth Plan deck.
- Best / Expected / Unexpected fill the deck's values; every field can be changed.
- "Lock 3,000 subscriptions" keeps subscriptions at the goal and calculates the budget needed.
- Works on phones: − / + buttons on every field and a summary bar that stays on top while scrolling.
